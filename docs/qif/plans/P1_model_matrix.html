Plan P1

The Model Matrix

Name every model the framework supports wholesale, record the architecture facts needed to price it, and expose the gaps that stand between the config schema and that list.

Mission context

The system runs ≤ 7B-total-parameter edge inference on analog FWS-CIM macro chiplets plus shared digital chiplets. Weights own cells, so the binding constraint is area and the binding number is TOTAL parameters (D2). This plan fixes the workload side of the contract: which models, what shape, what is missing.

The window is the last ~15 months, May 2025 – Aug 2026 (D3). It has been the window in which small models stopped being small transformers. The interesting half of the matrix is now state-space, linear-attention, and hybrid. That is where this plan spends its bandwidth.

Support is declared WHOLESALE, per model (D1). The op decomposition in the appendix is an implementation aid and never the support target.

Scope & exclusions

  • In: open-weights models at ≤ 7B total params — vision encoders, pure transformer LLMs (incl. GQA and MLA), SSM and hybrid models, small-TOTAL MoE.
  • Out by D2: big-total / small-active MoE. Nemotron 3 Nano (31.6B-A3B), Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, Gemma 4 26B-A4B, LFM2.5-8B-A1B (8.3B), Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B. These stay as architecture references only; they are exactly the wrong shape for FWS.
  • Out by D7: DiT / image diffusion, audio and speech. Gemma 4's audio encoder is ignored; the vision tower is not.
  • Address only (D8): VLM chaining, VLA. Named below, empty by design.
  • Not in scope here: accuracy. It is NEVER a factor anywhere in this project — performance model only (D23).

Where we stand

cim_timing.py (CimDeviceModel) holds every analog and fabric law. It prices ViT-class models and dense / MoE transformer LLMs with attention_type: mha or gqa, prefill and decode, with two KV stories. It rejects mla, and it has never seen a recurrence of any kind. docs/fws_cim/README.md lists MLA as a known future seam.

config.py defines the schema: LLMConfig, LLMAttentionConfig, MoEConfig, ViTConfig. model_type is one of gpt, llama, deepseek_v3, glm4_moe, vit, vit_dinov3. Notably, the MLA dims (kv_lora_rank, q_lora_rank, qk_nope_head_dim, qk_rope_head_dim, v_head_dim) are ALREADY parsed — MLA is a pricing gap, not a schema gap.

configs/model-config/ holds ~20 YAMLs: the ViT family (incl. vit_7b_patch16_dinov3_lvd1689m_inf.yaml), llama2_7b_fws_inf.yaml, moe_small_fws_inf.yaml, and the large MLA / MoE references DeepSeekV3_inf.yaml and GLM_4.5_AIR_106B_inf.yaml. Not one of them describes a recurrence. configs/model-config/hf_to_config.py converts a HuggingFace config.json into a Rapid-LLM YAML; it is the natural ingestion point for everything this plan adds.

The only existing SSM law anywhere in reach is OPTIMA's five-stage Mamba2 model, /app/nanocad/projects/cim_ctt_big_optima/perf_model/modeling/stages_mamba.py, described in docs/qif/plans/RECON_optima.md. Per docs/qif/plans/AUDIT_optima.md (D21) its stage laws are a numeric reference; nothing above them is.

Three findings the matrix forces

1. Pure transformer LLMs will lean on the shared digital chiplet, hard (D5). Attention and softmax are act×act and go to the shared digital chiplet (D13); only the weight GEMMs live in the macros. At ≤7B with long contexts the digital chiplet, not the macro array, sets the period. This is a finding to expose, never a blocker: every report carries the analog-vs-digital time and area split per model, so the shape of the answer is visible instead of hidden in a single number.

2. MLA is modelled, and it is ugly — show exactly how ugly (D6). Two things. First, no ≤7B open-weights MLA flagship exists in the window; MLA lives at 16B and above (Kimi K2.5, GLM-5, Ling 2.x, DeepSeek). The ≤7B carrier is MiniCPM3-4B, plus TransMLA-converted GQA models. Second, MLA is TP-hostile: the KV latent is a single shared rank, not a per-head slice, so a tp shard cannot split it. The up-projection weights are therefore REPLICATED on every shard, and under FWS weights are area. Since the array census already counts every shard at the full per-device figure, the cost lands directly on our weakest metric. P4 must print an explicit "area paid to KV replication" line rather than let it disappear into a total.

3. SSM state replaces the KV cache, and that moves the decode limiter. Today decode_throughput_limit on a dense LLM typically reports kv_capacity. An SSM layer carries a fixed state (d_inner × d_state) that does not grow with context; a 9:1 hybrid pays KV for four layers out of forty. Expect the limiter to move from kv_capacity to fabric on every hybrid row. That is the single strongest argument for the hybrids being the right workload for this machine, and it falls straight out of laws we already have.

Seams

Imports

  • P2 — device cards and tile semantics; whether the per-macro digital pool (D12) still absorbs helpers at the SSM elementwise ratio; bit-slicing cost for the narrow arrays the dt-projection and delta-rule gate projections produce.
  • P3 — op placement of scan / delta-rule / SWA onto the shared digital chiplet; tp / ep / pp shard-group legality per model shape; the PD-disagg prefill and decode spec pair (D16).
  • P4 — the report surfaces that carry this plan's three findings: analog-vs-digital split, MLA KV-replication area, decode limiter.
  • P6 — which matrix rows are validation targets. OPTIMA's Mamba2 stage laws are the only SSM reference that exists, and only at law level (D21).

Exports

  • The supported-model matrix: per-model architecture facts, support status, and priority.
  • The model-config schema growth list — block-typed layers, SSM params, linear-attention params, short conv, window pattern, per-block FFN dims, depth-shared weights.
  • The new-op inventory with a device assignment for each op (analog / per-macro pool / shared digital).
  • Three named findings the tool must expose: the analog:digital split, MLA KV-replication area, and the SSM-state-vs-KV decode limiter.
  • The D2 in/out boundary, including the borderline calls (Gemma 4 E4B raw 8B, RWKV-7 7.2B, LFM2.5-8B-A1B).
  • Address-only pointers for VLM chaining, PD disagg and VLA.

Implement now vs address only

Now. Block-typed layers with SSM, linear-attention and short-conv parameter groups in the schema and in hf_to_config.py. Mamba-2 SSD, the delta rule, sliding-window attention and short conv as priced ops on the heterogeneous device layer. The MLA pricing seam. The three findings as report lines. That covers every P0 row and most P1 rows.

Address only — VLM chaining (D8). The pairings the window actually produced are worth naming so the seam is not invented blind: Qwen3.5 with its own early-fusion 24-layer tower; Gemma 4 E-series with a ~150M vision encoder; Zamba2-VL 1.2B / 2.7B / 7B, which is a hybrid-SSM backbone on a vision tower; LFM2-VL; SmolVLM. Note that OPTIMA's "VLM" is weighted co-residency, not a chain (RECON §3) — there is nothing to port. Named, empty, no bandwidth now.

Address only — PD disaggregation (D16). The interesting pairing is the same model with GPU prefill and FWS decode. There is a real asymmetry in the handoff: a transformer hands over a KV cache whose bytes grow with S, while an SSM hands over a fixed-size state independent of S. A 9:1 hybrid therefore hands over a small constant plus KV for four layers out of forty — which makes hybrids the best-shaped workload for disaggregation in the whole matrix. Under D16 this is priced as bytes over the p2p law and nothing more; no precision conversion. Worth one experiment when P3 lands, not before.

Address only — VLA. Excluded from phase 1 (D7 removes the diffusion policy head; D8 defers the flow). VLA is latency-first and closed-loop and gets rethought when its time comes. The only obligation this plan carries is to keep the model schema free of assumptions that would need a rewrite — block-typed layers and per-op device placement are exactly the right shape for it (D22).

Open questions for George

  1. Gemma 4 E-series. 8B raw weights, 4.5B "effective" via Per-Layer Embeddings. D2 says total, and PLE weights must live in cells — my reading is E4B is OUT and E2B is IN. Confirm, because the same call governs any future MatFormer-style model.
  2. Hard cutoff or family exception? RWKV-7 7.2B and LFM2.5-8B-A1B are the flagships of in-scope families but sit just over 7B. List them as honorary, or exclude cleanly?
  3. How do we satisfy D6? No ≤7B MLA flagship exists in the window. Options: MiniCPM3-4B as the honorary carrier, a TransMLA-converted Llama config as a synthetic, or price MLA on a >7B model purely as an architecture study. I lean on the first two together.
  4. Diffusion language models. D7 excludes DiT and image diffusion. LLaDA-MoE-7B-A1B is a diffusion LM at exactly 7B total — in or out?
  5. Short depthwise conv placement. It is weight-stationary and tiny, which makes analog tempting, but depthwise wastes crossbar rows. Per-macro digital pool, or a narrow analog array priced honestly at bad utilization?
  6. Depth-shared weights (Zamba2). Express the share group in the model config, or let the mapper discover it? It is a genuine FWS area win either way; the question is who owns it, P1 or P3.
  7. The P0 headline model. Qwen3.5-4B (newest, in-window, edge-targeted, linear attention plus native vision) or Granite-4.0-H-Tiny (7B-A1B, sits exactly on the D2 corner and is the cleanest Mamba2 hybrid)? Both are P0; I want to know which one the first end-to-end run uses.
