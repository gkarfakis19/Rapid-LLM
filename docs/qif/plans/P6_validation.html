Plan P6

Validation

Which numbers are targets, which are provisional, and what the DAG-first evaluator must reproduce before it replaces the closed forms.

Mission context

The repo already validates. One script holds 152 gated checks and exits non-zero if any fails. The QIF build changes what is being validated, not how. Mapping is greenfield, the evaluator becomes DAG-first (A1), and OPTIMA stops being a single undifferentiated reference.

This page is a standing list, not a procedure (D18). It names what is gated, what is provisional, and what is never a target. It adds one new numeric check: the bridge from the closed forms to the DAG.

Scope & exclusions

In scope: the reference stance (D21), the bridge gate, the per-plan obligations, the honesty invariants, and the silicon calibration seam.

Out of scope:

  • Accuracy. NEVER a factor anywhere (D23).
  • Network validation. The network is fully-connected p2p with no congestion (A1, D17). AstraSim is off; nothing to compare against.
  • Multi-tenant and VLM parity. OPTIMA has no model to match there (D8, and AUDIT findings 2 and 3).
  • CI machinery, coverage targets, sign-off flow, schedules (D18).

The investigation

1. The stance (D21)

OPTIMA is not one thing. Each artifact carries its own status, permanently.

OPTIMA artifactStatusUse
Analog stage laws, per-stage byte flows, digital engine libraryReferenceParity target at law level. Spot-check against them; never port the surrounding machinery.
Array censuses, area and energy inputsReferenceInputs to our own census. Cross-check on dimensions.
Single-model recordings (ViT tiers; output/compiler_mamba_giant_22nm.md)TargetGated parity, single-model only.
_chip_accounting (fractional chips), the "mapper"Never a targetArea division, not placement. Nothing to match.
_apply_partial_bankingNever a targetAn aggregate smear that its own comment disclaims. Our banking is spatial and priced (D10).
_model_sequence_throughput, the multi-tenant layerNever a targetSerial time-sharing presented as concurrent throughput.
The combined-TOPS line in the multi-tenant reportNever quotableContradicts the SPS on the same page by ~10x. It is the reason for the one-accounting rule.
Proposal Table 1 VLM rows (_5nm_2d3d8, _5nm_max_perf)ProvisionalRan with max_link_bandwidth_GBs: 9999. Expect our model to correct them, not to match them.
A100 weight-streaming pathsPermanent gateBit-identical forever. Both baselines. No tolerance, no exceptions, no re-recording.

The asymmetry rule: when our number disagrees with a law-level reference, we are the suspect. When it disagrees with a provisional number, OPTIMA is.

2. The bridge gate

The DAG-first evaluator (P3 mapping, P4 pricing) replaces the closed-form pipeline report. It must first reproduce it. Run the evaluator on the degenerate case — one model, dedicated macros, one owner per macro, no column sharing, no bit-slicing, no PD split — and compare against the pass-1/2 closed-form fws_cim numbers on the configs both paths express.

QuantityCriterionWhy
Stage set and op count per layerExactDiscrete structure. A mismatch is a mapping bug.
QK and PV cyclesExactInteger cycle counts from the same folded law.
Arrays per layer, chips, macro censusExactIntegers by invariant (D21).
Bottleneck stage identityExactA name, not a number.
Period, block latency, end-to-end latency≤ 0.1 % relativeContinuous; the DAG sums per-op durations while the closed form takes a max, so float paths differ.
fps, tok/s, sustained tok/s≤ 0.1 % relativeDerived from the above.
Area, energy per token, boundary transfer times≤ 0.1 % relativeSame law, different summation order.

Tolerance philosophy, mirroring the existing validator: exact where the quantity is discrete, tight-relative at REL_TOL = 1e-3 where it is continuous. Where a law can be restated in a few lines, recompute it independently inside the check rather than comparing the evaluator to itself. That is the pass-2A spot-check pattern.

What the gate deliberately excludes: fill and drain, tails, contention, banking, bit-slicing, PD handoff, and heterogeneous placement. The closed form cannot express any of them (A1, D22). Disagreement there is the new content, not a failure. The gate covers the intersection only, and the closed-form report survives as a legacy reference precisely to hold it.

3. Per-plan obligations

PlanWhat validation means there
P1 — Model MatrixNew models have no hardware truth. Validation is internal consistency: parameter count derived from config dims matches the published total and sits under 7B (D2); census and dimension cross-checks hold (array counts follow from dims; mux divides heads where a law demands it). Published-throughput figures may be cited as sanity references and must be labeled non-binding on the page that shows them.
P2 — Macro Resource ModelLaw spot-checks against recorded OPTIMA points, including the analog-time digital-clock quantization with its +1 cycle. Physics audits in the arithmetic-peak style at every law change: a law that exceeds a declared peak is wrong even when parity passes (DESIGN2 §8 precedent). New pricing proves itself by degenerate reduction — slicing off and pool unbounded reproduce today's numbers bit-identically.
P3 — MappingPlacement validity invariants, checked on the mapping and not on a number: chips are integers; every macro has a named owner; capacity is respected (tiles never oversubscribe columns × mux slots); every constraint relaxation is disclosed in the artifact that used it.
P4 — EvaluationMetric honesty: one accounting per metric per report. No metric appears twice computed two ways. Any relaxed constraint is named in the report that benefits from it. P4 also owns the bridge gate above.
P5 — System AtlasThe atlas renders exactly the mapping data. No geometry, count, or grouping is invented in the view. Owners, chip counts, and shard-group colors are read from the mapping record; a macro absent from the mapping is absent from the atlas.

4. The silicon calibration seam

Samyak's measured macro data replaces card parameters — energy per vector, area per array, geometry, bits-per-cell, timing constants. It does not touch laws. The device card is the only surface that changes (D14).

Three consequences. OPTIMA parity rows stay pinned to the OPTIMA card and stay frozen. Measured-silicon runs are a different card and a different set of numbers; the two never share a report. If measured data contradicts a law's shape rather than its constants, that is a law change: it takes a physics audit and a re-run of the bridge gate.

Seams

Imports

  • P1 — model configs and their declared dimension and parameter facts, for the consistency cross-checks.
  • P2 — the laws and device cards under spot-check, plus the degenerate-reduction switches (slicing off, pool unbounded).
  • P3 — the mapping record: owners, integer chip counts, capacity usage, disclosed relaxations.
  • P4 — the DAG evaluator's report and its metric list, for the bridge gate.
  • P5 — the atlas input, which is the same mapping record, for the render-fidelity check.

Exports

  • The stance table: every OPTIMA artifact classified as target, reference, provisional, or never.
  • The bridge gate: its degenerate case, its scope, and its exact-vs-relative tolerance rule.
  • The honesty invariants (D21) as checkable statements, each with an owning plan.
  • The frozen A100 bit-identity baselines and the frozen ViT tier targets.
  • The calibration rule: cards change, laws do not.

Open questions for George

  1. Bridge-gate breadth: are the three ViT tiers plus the Llama7b point enough, or must every shipped config be expressible in both paths?
  2. Is 1e-3 the right bar for the closed-form-to-DAG period comparison, or should the degenerate case be held tighter?
  3. When the two paths disagree outside fill and drain, which one is the erratum? Default proposed: the DAG wins and the closed form is annotated, since it is legacy (A1).
  4. Table 1's VLM rows: publish our corrected figures as a correction of record, or drop the comparison entirely?
  5. Does the closed-form path stay in the tree indefinitely to hold the bridge gate, or is it frozen to recorded artifacts once the gate passes?
